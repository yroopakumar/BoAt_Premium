01 · The challenge

Make it premium — without ever
compromising reliability.

The brief asked for a companion app people open daily, with moments of delight, resilient when hardware or connectivity falters, and valuable enough to pay for. Choosing which journeys to design — and defending the choice — was part of the test.

The experience snapshot
  • Setup — Pairing works reliably; most complete onboarding
  • Daily use — People open briefly to check vitals, then leave
  • Coming back — Many stop opening after the first few weeks
  • Look & feel — Functional, sometimes great; needs newer trends

“…without ever compromising the reliability of the device-app experience.”

Why it matters to the business
#1in Indian wearables — 29.2% share (Q2 2026)
#2in smartwatches — 15.2% share, behind Noise
₹901 → ₹330 CrboAt wearables revenue, FY23 → FY25
10 quartersof falling smartwatch shipments — yet price per watch is up 24%
The reframe

After purchase, the app is the only daily conversation boAt has with an owner.

So the question wasn't “how do we make it prettier?”
It was “why does the conversation go quiet?”

02 · Discovery

People don't dislike the app.
Trust breaks at specific moments.

With no primary research in scope, I went to the most honest public evidence: boAt's own support pages, live store reviews, IDC market data, and Indian user and sleep surveys.

Trust

boAt's own support guide

“Sync the watch with the app in the morning to view sleep data.” Stuck sync → unbind and reconnect.

Trust

Live store signal

Android 4.5★ · 1.74 lakh reviews · 1 crore+ installs. Recent reviews: sync failures, OTP login blocking pairing, step data wiped.

Meaning

First-time, doubtful owners

76% of young Indian users doubt their watch's accuracy; ~80% are on their first smartwatch (CMR 2024).

Ritual

A country that sleeps late

60% sleep after 11 PM. Bengaluru: 54% wake unrested, 61.7% sleepy at work (Wakefit 2026).

Reliability

Phones that kill the app

41% of 2025 Indian phones come from brands rated 4–5/5 for killing background apps.

Value

Gen Z already pays

Gen Z are 75% of boAt's AI fitness plan & coach subscribers (boAt Crest Data Trend 2025).

The insight

The villain isn't an ugly screen.
The villain is doubt.

If Ananya can't trust that a number is fresh, a beautiful number is worthless. So premium had to start with trust.

Ananya, 28 · Bengaluru

Mid-range Redmi phone · boAt watch · checks her sleep at 6:42 before work · wakes tired · half-doubts the numbers.

Composite built from the evidence above, not interview data.

03 · Defining premium

Premium became four behaviours
I could test on every screen.

Calm

One answer at a time. No alarm-red for normal variation.

Confident

Freshness always visible. Failures explained and self-healed.

Personal

Her baseline, her best nights, her words — never averages.

Crafted

Purposeful motion and haptics, still smooth on a ₹12,000 phone.

05 · Choosing what to design

Twelve journeys. Two that move
every number the business cares about.

I mapped the lifecycle — unbox, pair, first night, daily glance, week 2–6 habit, upgrade, lapse — and scored each journey on user pain × business value × premium potential × reliability risk.

J1

Daily glance → insight

The 6:42 moment. Fresh, trusted, one answer. Every user, every day — where trust is won or lost.

J2

Weeks 2–6 · habit + Plus preview

Where D30 retention (3–8% in category) collapses — and where a paid tier earns its right to exist.

We chose X over Y

No to onboarding redesign

Pairing pain is mostly engineering — sync and OTP. I folded its states into J1 instead.

No to a watch face store

Visible, but it doesn't fix trust or retention. Phase 3 at the earliest.

No to a social feed

High build cost, low fit for a calm, private health product.

07 · Journey 2 · Weeks 2–6 · Habit + Plus preview

Earn the habit first.
Then offer the coach.

A Sunday Weekly Review turns seven nights into one story. In week 2, Plus appears as a preview of her own data — never a wall. Vitals stay free forever; ₹99/month via UPI Autopay only once value is felt.

Free, forever
  • All vitals & history
  • Day Score + one-line insight
  • Sync health & self-heal
  • Weekly Review
boAt Plus · ₹99/mo
  • Personal coaching plans
  • Deeper trends & what-ifs
  • Cycle-aware & goal-based guidance
  • Evolves boAt's existing AI plans

09 · Value added & future

Personalisation that respects the person.

Goal-based segments

Sleep better, lose weight, train for a 10K — tips and targets change with the goal she picks.

Evidence: 76% doubt accuracy → meaning, not more metrics

Cycle-aware women's health

Opt-in cycle logging; energy, sleep and workout guidance adapt by phase. Never medical claims.

Evidence: boAt Lunar Vista already tracks cycles · DPDP consent

Gen Z fitness

Challenges, shareable weekly story, AI coach on Plus.

Evidence: Gen Z = 75% of boAt AI fitness subscribers

10 · Measuring success & rollout

How we'd know it worked.

North star

Trusted daily opens

Opens where data was fresh (< 2 h) — trust × habit.

Reliability

Sync success rate

Silent-failure rate ↓ · reconnect via self-heal ↑

Retention

D30 / D60

Category D30 is 3–8%; target set after a baseline.

Business

Plus trial → paid

Without hurting store rating or free retention.

Rollout: 5% staged release on Android first (where 4–5/5 battery-killing OEMs dominate) → A/B the morning reveal and Plus preview → expand when sync success and rating hold. Targets are placeholders until boAt's analytics give a baseline.

11 · Reflection
What I'd do with more time
  • Validate with 8–10 owners on mid-range Android
  • Prototype motion on a ₹12,000 phone
  • Design the ninth state: OTP-blocked pairing
What I learned
  • Premium is a promise kept, not a gradient
  • Reliability is a design problem, with engineering
  • Saying no is the lead's real deliverable
